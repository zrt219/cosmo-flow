### 🔄 Post 10: Runge-Kutta (RK4) Numerical Velocity Vectors
**Media File**: `ad-10-rk4-streamlines-velocity-flow.gif`  
**Target Group**: Math Devs, Engine Programmers, Computer Science Students

```markdown
Euler vs. 4th-Order Runge-Kutta in Astronomical Flow Simulations 🧮

When tracing streamlines across 500 million light-years, standard 1st-order Euler integration causes truncation error drift that breaks watershed boundaries:
$x(t + dt) = x(t) + v(x) \cdot dt$ ❌

CosmoFlow uses 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4):
$k_1 = v(x)$
$k_2 = v(x + \frac{dt}{2} k_1)$
$k_3 = v(x + \frac{dt}{2} k_2)$
$k_4 = v(x + dt \cdot k_3)$
$x(t + dt) = x(t) + \frac{dt}{6} (k_1 + 2k_2 + 2k_3 + k_4)$ ✅

The result: Mathematically rigorous streamlines that conserve energy and convergence across the cosmic basin.

Explore the math in 3D:
👉 https://epic-hubble.vercel.app
⭐ Star on GitHub: https://github.com/zrt219/cosmo-flow
```
